Match your resume

Upload your resume to find the listings from the last few weeks that fit it best.

Your resume is not stored anywhere. It is read once to make the match, then discarded and never saved to disk, our database, or our logs. Matching needs a free account, which keeps your email address only for the reminders you ask for.

To work out your skills and level, the resume is sent to Google's Gemini API on a paid plan, under which Google does not use it to train its models. Results are ranked by score = cosine similarity + 0.03 if the listing's category fits your resume, − 0.06 if its title is clearly too senior or too junior for you.

    Method and caveats

    Collection. Every night at midnight a worker queries public job boards through JobSpy and stores what it finds in SQLite.

    The forecast. a non-seasonal ARIMA(p, d, q), with the differencing order chosen by an augmented Dickey Fuller test and the rest by AICc. It is fitted in log space and damped, so it flattens instead of running off the chart. The band is a 95% prediction interval.
